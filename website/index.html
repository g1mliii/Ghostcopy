Your desk
01

One keystroke is the whole interface.

Ctrl + Shift + S opens a 500 pixel window over whatever you were doing. Type or paste, press Enter, it is gone again. There is no main window to go back to, because there isn’t one.

02

Sealed before it leaves the room.

Set a passphrase and the key is derived on your device and never leaves it. There is no cloud backup of it, no recovery link, nothing we could be compelled to produce.

A second device gets the key by scanning a QR code off the first one. That is the only way, on purpose.

03

Asleep until you need it.

While the window is hidden every animation and stream is paused — not throttled, paused. The only thing still listening is the hotkey. Idle cost is 0.0 % CPU, which is the entire reason it can sit in your tray forever.

04

And it is in your pocket.

Tap the notification and the text is on the phone’s clipboard. Ignore it and the clip is waiting in the home screen widget anyway.

Going the other way is paste‑then‑send, because both mobile systems block background clipboard reads — and they should.
