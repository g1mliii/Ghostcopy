GhostCopy

Your clips. Your choices.

GhostCopy sends a clip to your other linked devices when you choose to send it. Do not send passwords, payment details or someone else’s private information.

If you are 5–17, ask a parent or guardian to help you set up. We ask for your age and country, not your birthday. Your parent must finish adult verification and allow cloud sync before your account starts. Parent setup is not available until our verification service is ready. Children under 5 cannot use cloud accounts.

Your clips are locked with a passphrase on your device before they are sent. Keep that passphrase safe and share it only with the trusted person helping you set up your other devices. GhostCopy cannot recover it.

We store your locked clips and information needed to deliver them, such as your device names. Your parent can see which features are allowed, ask for saved account data, stop sync or delete your cloud account. Reading locked clips needs the passphrase.

We do not turn on automatic sending, optional crash reports, rating requests or targeted advertising for parent-managed accounts. Only send clips you want to share.

You can delete clips or your account in Settings. Ask your parent for help if you do not understand a choice or want syncing to stop. Deleting cloud data does not erase something already pasted or saved on another device.

Need help? Ask your parent to contact support@ghostcopy.app.
