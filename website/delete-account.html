Delete your account, and everything in it.

GhostCopy · Last updated 22 September 2026

In the app

01

Open GhostCopy on your iPhone, iPad or Android phone, signed in to the account you want to delete.

02

Open Settings and tap Delete Account.

03

Confirm. If you signed in with Apple, you will also confirm with Apple, which lets us revoke GhostCopy’s access to your Apple ID.

What is deleted

Your account, your whole clipboard history, every file and image you sent (they are removed from storage, not just hidden), the devices linked to the account and their push tokens, and the cloud backup of your passphrase if you made one. It happens at once, and it cannot be undone.

What stays

Anything already on your own devices: text you pasted, files you saved, and clips a webhook or the Obsidian integration already delivered to a place you control. Database backups taken before the deletion are never used to restore it and expire on our database provider’s rolling schedule.

Without the app

Email support@ghostcopy.app from the address on the account and ask us to delete it. We reply to confirm, then delete everything listed above. A guest account that was never signed in to is deleted automatically after 90 days without use.
