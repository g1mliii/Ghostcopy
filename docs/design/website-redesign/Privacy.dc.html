What we hold, and what we cannot.

PRIVACY POLICY · LAST UPDATED [DATE YOU PUBLISH THIS]

IN SHORT

We hold the minimum needed to move a clip from one of your devices to another. If you set a passphrase, the clip is encrypted before it reaches us and we cannot read it. Nothing is sold, and there is no advertising or analytics SDK in the app or on this site.

WHAT WE COLLECT

An account is created anonymously the first time you open the app, identified by a random ID and nothing else. You only give us an email address if you choose to upgrade the account, or sign in with Google — in which case we receive the address on that Google account.

For each clip you send we store its content, the type of device that sent it, the name you gave that device, and the time. Where encryption is on, the content field holds ciphertext. We also store one push notification token per mobile device, which is what lets a clip reach your phone.

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics. If you join the beta list we store the email address you typed, and nothing alongside it.

WHAT WE NEVER SEE
Your passphrase
Never transmitted, never backed up. Moving it to a new device happens directly between your devices by QR code.
Encrypted clip contents
With a passphrase set, plaintext never leaves your device in either direction.
Anything unsent
The desktop app does not watch your clipboard in the background. Nothing exists until you send it.
THE CAVEAT
Without a passphrase, encryption is off and clip content is stored in plain text on our infrastructure. Row‑level security still restricts it to your account, but we could technically read it.
WHO PROCESSES IT
Supabase
Hosts the database, your account, and the realtime connection that delivers clips.
Firebase Cloud
Messaging
Delivers push notifications to Android and iOS. Receives the notification payload and your device token.
Cloudflare
Serves this website. It does not touch clip data at all.
HOW LONG WE KEEP IT

Clips stay until you delete them or delete your account. Deleting a clip removes the row; there is no archive copy. Deleting your account removes the account and every clip attached to it. Backups taken before a deletion roll off on their own schedule and are not queryable by us in the ordinary course.

YOUR RIGHTS

You can ask for a copy of what is stored against your account, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete all of it — email us from the address on the account. Note the obvious limit: for encrypted clips an export gives you ciphertext, because that is genuinely all we hold.
